# Design System: Arulraj S — Sovereign AI Builder & Digital Studio (2026 Edition)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A cutting-edge, ultra-clean, high-density yet breathable digital studio aesthetic. Inspired by top-tier modern tech founders, Linear, Vercel, and Stripe. Built for commercial buyers, business owners, and founders who want an elite engineer who can build and scale their web products autonomously.

- **Theme**: Dark Graphite / Obsidian Minimalist with Electric Lime Accents
- **Canvas Base**: `#0C0D0E` (Ultra-deep, warm obsidian neutral, never pitch `#000000`)
- **Card Surfaces**: `#141618` (Subtle tactile dark zinc)
- **Elevated Hover Surface**: `#1A1D20`
- **Primary Text**: `#EDEDEC` (Crisp readability, high contrast)
- **Muted Text**: `#9CA3AF` (Secondary metadata, comfortable eye-rest)
- **Subtle Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Accent Punch**: `#C8F169` (Electric Lime for active states, conversion badges, and highlights)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#3B82F6` (Electric Blue for tech tags) and `#10B981` (Emerald for live systems)

## 2. Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Outfit` / `Manrope`, sans-serif. Tight tracking (`-0.035em`), bold weight (`700`, `800`).
- **Body**: `Inter`, sans-serif (`400`, `500`). Max line length: 65ch. Line height: 1.65.
- **Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` / `Fira Code` for code badges, status tags, and statistics.

## 4. Interaction & Motion Rules
- Subtle glowing background mesh (`opacity: 0.4`, hardware accelerated).
- Tactile card micro-elevation (`translateY(-4px)` with soft diffused glow).
- Real screenshots in clean browser mockups.
- Mobile drawer navigation with zero layout shift.
- Instant 1-click WhatsApp routing for client inquiries.
